Analysis

Thou! Thou Thou!

Strengths

  • Very diverse, with:
  • Great set of physical Rages, healing Rages, AoE damage & control, & debuffs
  • Good set of elemental control & buffs
  • Secondary Healer; has Rerise & Harvester
  • Speed +2 esper
  • Good elemental resistances, Unique Hide/Muffler armor
  • Unique mage helmet
  • Good p.eva

Weaknesses

  • Proper grammar & pronunciation
  • Rages are many & highly specialized with no in-game reference sheet
  • Rage is untargetable & a little random
  • No HP espers, no heavy armor, low p.def
  • Mid-game damage is flaky if no Bone (with no Blizzard Orb, no speed esper)
  • Switching Rages mid-battle is awkward; Gau can't gear for both Vigor & Magic
  • Speed is unavailable until the WoR

The finer details of Gau's abilities require a lot of memorization, both of Rage & of enemy encounters. But, even with just a few Rages, Gau makes a great speedy glass cannon & healer/buffer.

Builds

go for 1100+ hp, focus EL down one path, focus on a primary damage type, don't neglect defense and auto-status

Berserker

The standard Gau build, raw speed, with or without a little spirit. Biggest downside is that it's not available until the WoR. Turns Gau into a living bullet, allowing him to really lay on the damage or healing despite having "lower" numbers than other characters, spam buffs like Witch or Conjurer, or open up randoms with strong debuffs. Even the healing from his spirit-based Rages is still pretty good, thanks to how many turns Gau gets relative to everyone else.

Spirit

The other build, available from the WoB, built with mostly spirit levels (though not purely with spirit). The main sell of the this build is higher numbers on the Rocky & Mesosaur Rages, as opposed to Rageserker's higher volume of heals. It also boasts some nice, all-around spirit damage Rages (Exocite, Cephalid, Mantodea), as well as spirit's other utility benefits. All in all, this build is perhaps a little easier to use than Rageserker, but has much less potential. The spirit-damage Rages are high damage, versatile, & easy to use, but Rageserker's raw speed will win out once it's got a good Rage or two backing it (see the Simple set of Rages below). Also, speed is just as needed for the healing Rages as spirit is, considering Gau's random nature. For the WoB, Gau's spirit-attack Rages (Exocite & Cephalid) are his best offensive Rages, so this build is good for the WoB. It just falls behind speed Gau once the mid-WoR hits.

Mixed

The mixed build. Probably the build you're using if you use Gau in the WoB, because pure spirit isn't worthwhile.

Good Rages

Simple

Hornet, Doggo, Templar, Werewolf, and Tyrano are the main physical Rages. Tumbleweed: (Cure 3) Ribbon resistances, auto-Regen, and a rare elemental resistance. Mesosaur, Rhydon and Rocky are also good. Conjurer: Rerise spam can win fights. (Hornet) and Witch (+Locust/Troll) have some utility for setting Image. Albatross: Solid early-game AoE & sap setter. Windrunner is the WoR AoE & sap setter. Titan: Solid ST damage & Sap setter for WoR. Griffin is a good alternative. Antlion: Stop/Snare is a vicious combo.

Extra

Fire: Lich & Albatross. WoR follows up with not-quite-as-effective Shokan, Witch, and the undead-killing Revenant. Bolt: Rainman in the WoB, Griffin in the WoR. Also, Anemone. Water: Vaporite in the WoB, Ninja, Vagrant. Dark: Sewer Rat and Shokan/Marlboro. Other AoEs: Leafer/Sand Ray/Stray Cat/Crawler (early), Vulture (mid), Scarab (late), Weedula/Belladonna (tricky) Status Control: Exocite/Rocky (muddle), Marlboro (several), Allosaurus (physical, several)

Misc

Don't forget Gau's Magic. HasteX & Fenrir are especially good as opening salvos for a boss fight. His other Magics also have their utility. Grab a Magic Bone asap. The BPow and Magic+7 are critical to Gau's damage in the WoR. A Blizzard Orb is helpful as well. Black Heart combined with the right shield, hide, and Rage can make Gau quite bulky. It's even compatible with Harvester spam! Especially useful if you snag the hidden WoB Heart, or for anytime prior to earning the Snow Muffler. Use Green Cherry or kill it to switch Rage.
