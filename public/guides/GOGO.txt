ANALYSIS

He banished himself from FFV and found himself in FFVI.

Strengths

Very versatile support skill set
Good AoE / debuff / crowd controll skill set
Rod + Rage is good synergy for raw damage
Decent mg.def with Hide / Muffler armors
Decent speed gear options
Mimic

Weaknesses

Bad stats with no esper growths
Poor HP, no heavy armor
Magic is dependent on team composition
Damage set-up (Rod + Rage) is untargetable & a little random
Gogo's role on the team is as a semi-speedy, frail, all-around support guy who can choose to go full ham with damage. 
He also has a very wide range of minor application. He's honestly quite similar to Strago, just less powerful but 
more diverse. Another good comparison is that he's a weaker but more controllable Gau.

SET-UPS

- Support

Gear Gogo for speed, pick 3 of Tools, Runic, Throw, Lore, Magic (with a proper team), Steal, or Item. Maybe even 
Slots (Go Fish) or Blitz (Chakra). Also has some niche uses for Fight with Brush (heal) or Rod (elemental sniping). 
Solid support character. Mimic can be used to copy a teammate's attack on any off-turns; just be aware that you 
don't Mimic Rage / Dance, lest you lose control of Gogo.

Noticeable characteristics include being a:

3rd MP Battery
2nd Runic user
3rd (reliable) Image user whose also the 2nd (reliable) Image user with a shield
2nd Shield user
4th Life 2 / Defib user
Tools, Lore, & Magic could also give him some AoE debuffs / crowd control for randoms. Cleave (Bushido) or Schock 
with speed gear is also a great random clearer, though these skills do little else and are incompatible with Rod/Brushes.

- Rod Rage

Gear for mag/spd, Equip Rod, pick Rage, blow something up. Your options are Templar (3x damage, innate amulet/ribbon/safe/shell), 
Adamantite (Safe and Holy Wind), Tyrano (blocks earth and wind, status immune), Hornet (Innate Haste/float, 1/3 Image), 
Troll (1/3 refract) or Scrapper (1/3 Chakra). While the physical hit itself won't be too amazing, the random 
spellcast will do absurd damage, possibly hitting 9999 even without a weakness. You do need to be in the front row 
for maximum damage, so it's a glass cannon set-up. Rage also gives a few other options for AoE control (Ninja, Vulture, Windrunner) 
or healing (Tumbleweed, Rocky), so there's some flexibility between fights here. If you equip a knife in the offhand, 
you can equip Schock or Bushido (for Eclipse) as your second Command for solid all-around offense. Fill the other two 
commands with whatever other utility you want: Magic, Item, Tools, Throw, Lore, Steal, Slots, Blitz (chakra), or 
Runic (with a knife in the off-hand).
