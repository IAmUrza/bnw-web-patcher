A challenging mode that owes its name to thzfunnyman's Brave New World spinoff, Final Frontier.

1. Caps character levels at 30 and esper levels at 20.
2. Weakness to an element will result in a 150% damage bonus, down from 200%.
3. Reduces the HP and MP gained from Esper Levels.
4. Caps each inventory stack at a maximum of 20 items, instead of 99.
